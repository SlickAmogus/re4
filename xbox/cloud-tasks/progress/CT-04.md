- tools/drs.py: hakase container = 0x20 sig + 32-byte BE records (type,size,0,offset,p0,p1,0,0) term 0xFFFFFFFF, table zero to 0x400;
  rec0 type0 body at 0x400; rec1 type4 sound bank or 0xFFFFFFFE (size0, offset=file size). Body: u32 count, rel_offset,0,0;
  u32 ofs[count]; char tag[count][4]; entries 0x20-aligned, no sizes (run to next ofs). REL at body+rel_offset (rel_size via relfile).
  drs.Drs(d) parses; .entries [(tag4,bytes)], .rel, .snd (SoundBank .records (type,p0,p1,blob)); pack() byte-identical.
- tools/motion/archive.py: load_das(path) -> (Drs, DasInfo) decompressing yz2 body; parse_body(body,endian) for bare .dat
  (u32 count,0,0,0; ofs[]; tags[]); parse_event(d) 'event' header, nBin/binOfs at 0x48 (>iI), EvtBinEntry 0x40 = name[0x30], u32 ofs,size;
  parse_etm(d) EtcArc: u32 num; files from 0x20 {u32 size;..;name[0x20]@0x20; data@0x40}; eff_motions(d) EFF version 0xB,
  ofs tables at 0x14 and 0x2C; smd_motions. EXT_TAG maps .fcv/.seq/.bin/.tpl/.eff/.mdt/.lit/.fcs for named evd/ETM entries.
  Game addresses entry as PL_ARC_PTR(arc,no)=arc->ofs[no]+arc (index+4), so the body header words must stay in place (swap in place).
